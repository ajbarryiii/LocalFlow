Autocorrect spelling tables (autocorrect/spelling.lfwe)

The keyboard's autocorrect spelling channel uses a table of per-letter
spelling-error probabilities (substitution, omission, insertion and
transposition rates). Only these aggregated probabilities ship; no word,
sentence or other text from the sources below is included.

1. Wikipedia, "Lists of common misspellings/For machines"
   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Lists_of_common_misspellings/For_machines
   Revision 1199637275 (2024-01-27), by Wikipedia contributors.
   Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
   International License (CC BY-SA 4.0):
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
   Changes: the list's misspelling and correction pairs were aligned letter
   by letter and counted into smoothed edit probabilities. The tables are
   an adaptation of that list and are shared under the same licence.

2. The Aalto typing37k dataset
   https://userinterfaces.aalto.fi/typing37k/
   Kseniia Palin, Anna Maria Feit, Sunjun Kim, Per Ola Kristensson and
   Antti Oulasvirta. 2019. How do People Type on Mobile Devices?
   Observations from a Study with 37,000 Volunteers. In Proceedings of the
   21st International Conference on Human-Computer Interaction with Mobile
   Devices and Services (MobileHCI '19). ACM.
   https://doi.org/10.1145/3338286.3340120
   The edit probabilities count the words participants typed against the
   sentences they were shown.
