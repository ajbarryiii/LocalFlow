Privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026 · Applies to Sorello for iPhone, previously named LocalFlow for iOS.

On-device dictation

Sorello performs English speech recognition on your device using a model bundled with the app. It does not upload your recordings or transcripts to the developer or to a transcription service. No account is required. Sorello includes no advertising, tracking, or analytics SDK.

Microphone and keyboard permissions

Microphone permission is needed to capture speech you choose to dictate. During a session you explicitly start, iOS displays its microphone indicator while the microphone remains ready. Audio received between dictations is discarded. You can end the session in Sorello; sessions also end after their configured idle period.

The keyboard's Full Access permission allows it to communicate with the containing app through a shared container on your device. Sorello does not use this permission to send typed text, recordings, or transcripts to a server. iOS uses its system keyboard for secure fields, and the app where you insert text controls its own handling of that text.

Local storage

Dictation audio is processed in memory. Settings, the words you add to your dictionary, and model preparation files are stored locally. A sentence you record to find a dictionary word's strength is processed in memory and discarded right after. If you choose Never correct for a word, that word is stored on your device until you clear it in Diagnostics. The app and keyboard briefly share dictation results in a local cache to insert text; results are removed when handled or cleaned up after expiry. Sorello does not maintain a transcript history. Text inserted into another app is subject to that app's storage and privacy practices. Deleting Sorello removes its local app data, subject to iOS handling of shared app containers and backups.

Optional support email

Report a bug previews a draft and opens your email app only when you choose Write email. It includes the displayed app version, iOS version, generic device type, and reproduction prompts. It attaches no recordings, transcripts, logs, or other files automatically. You may edit or cancel the draft, and sending requires a separate action in your email app.

If you send a support email, the developer receives the sender address and the contents you choose to include. Support mail is routed by Cloudflare Email Routing and delivered through Google's email service. These providers process the message under their own privacy policies. Support information is used to respond to your request and investigate the reported issue; it is not used for advertising or tracking. You can request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing the address below.

External links and Apple services

If you open a support or privacy-policy link, your browser connects to the website hosting that page, which may receive ordinary connection information. Apple may separately process App Store or TestFlight information according to your Apple settings and Apple's policies. Sorello does not operate a dictation server.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or support correspondence deletion requests, email localflow@wildernesslabs.ai. Changes to the app's privacy practices will be reflected in this policy and, where required, the App Store privacy disclosure.
