Support

Sorello is an offline English dictation app and custom keyboard for iPhone, requiring iOS 26 or later. It was previously called LocalFlow.

Setup

  1. Open Sorello and complete onboarding, including microphone permission and local model preparation.
  2. In Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards, tap Add New Keyboard and choose Sorello. Then tap Sorello and turn on Allow Full Access so the keyboard can communicate with the app on your device.
  3. In a text field, for example in Notes, switch to Sorello with the globe key.
  4. Tap Start. If no session is running, Sorello opens to start one; return to your text field as prompted and speak. Tap Stop to insert the text.
  5. While the session is on, Start dictates without opening Sorello again. Tap End session in Sorello when you are finished.

Common questions

Why is the microphone indicator still on? An active session keeps the microphone ready. Audio between dictations is discarded. End session stops capture; a session also ends after it has been idle for the length set in Sorello, 5 minutes by default.

Why is the keyboard unavailable? Password and certain other fields use the system keyboard. Apps can also disable custom keyboards.

Why is there no inserted text? Check microphone permission and Full Access, complete model preparation, and try a short dictation in Notes. Locking during dictation cancels that dictation.

Report a problem

In Sorello, choose Report a bug > Write email. Or email localflow@wildernesslabs.ai directly. Include the steps to reproduce, expected result, actual result, app version, iOS version, and device type. Avoid including passwords, sensitive text, or personal recordings. No recordings, transcripts, or logs are attached by the app automatically.
